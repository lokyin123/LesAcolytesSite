We are thrilled to share that, following an audition process, Les Acolytes were selected as Brighton Early Music Festival Emerging Artists in May 2026. Together with three other ensembles, we will be joining this year-long scheme, run by BREMF with its professional partners.

[Brighton Early Music Festival’s Emerging Artists scheme](https://www.bremf.org.uk/emerging-artists/) is one of the pre-eminent mentoring and development schemes for emerging professional early music performers in the UK. The year will include performances, professional development opportunities, and education and outreach work with BREMF’s partners in the South of England.

Our involvement with the Festival will begin with a residency at Hawkwood College in Gloucestershire, followed by showcase performances in October. Read more about our upcoming performances on the [What’s On](/whats-on/) page.

We are very grateful to [BREMF](https://www.bremf.org.uk/) for offering us this opportunity, and we look forward to sharing more about the year ahead.
