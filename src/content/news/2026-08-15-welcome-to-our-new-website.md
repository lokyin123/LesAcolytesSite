We are excited to present our new website, built in-house over the past few weeks.

Here you can read about the ensemble and its musicians, explore our recordings and programmes, and find details of upcoming performances.

As the website is still new, some sections will be under construction. We will be gradually adding more videos, programmes, and events. We will also be updating this News section with more announcements and posts, so please do check back later for more!

You can also keep up with us by following us on **[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lesacolytes.uk/)** and subscribing to our **[YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@lesacolytesuk)**.

The website was built and is managed in-house by our flautist, Billy. We have worked to make it as accessible as possible, but if we have missed anything, or if you encounter a bug (!), we would be grateful if you let us know. You can **[contact us](/contact/)** at **[info@lesacolytes.uk](mailto:info@lesacolytes.uk)**.

Thank you for stopping by, and we hope you enjoy looking around!
