Les Acolytes was formed in 2025 by four musicians who share a fascination with the colour, detail and conversation at the heart of French Baroque music.

Andrew, from Glasgow, plays the violin; Billy, from Hong Kong, plays the traverso; Tim, from Taiwan, plays the viola da gamba; and Edd, from the North East of England, plays the harpsichord.

To learn more about the ensemble, visit our [About](/about/) page. You can also meet each of us on the [Musicians](/musicians/) page. Elsewhere on the website, you can explore our programmes and recordings, and see where we will be performing.

We look forward to sharing more of our work here. To keep up with us, follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/lesacolytes.uk/) and subscribe to our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@lesacolytesuk).
