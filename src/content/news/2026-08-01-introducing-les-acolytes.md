Les Acolytes is a Cambridge-based chamber ensemble devoted to French Baroque cantatas and related repertoire. We bring together musicians who share a fascination with the colour, detail and conversation at the heart of this music.

The ensemble performs on historical instruments and builds programmes around the music we most enjoy discovering together. This website is a place to find out more about the musicians, explore our programmes and recordings, and see where we will be performing.

We look forward to sharing more of our work here.
